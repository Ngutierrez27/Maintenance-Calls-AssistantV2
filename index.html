📞 Call Handling Script

Starting the Call

“Thank you for calling the maintenance line. This is [name], How may I assist you today?”

Assessing the Situation

“I’m very sorry to hear that. Can I get a little more information before we get started?”

Collecting Information

  • Unit Number
  • Community Name
  • Full Name
  • Contact Number

Determining the Emergency

“May I place you on a brief hold while I document that?”

Mitigating / Solving the Issue

Emergency: “Let’s go over a few things to help before escalation.”

Non-Emergency: “Per protocol, this isn’t an emergency. I’d be happy to assist now.”

Ending the Call

Emergency: “Technician will call you within the hour.”

Non-Emergency: “Work order submitted. We’ll follow up during business hours.”

Resolved: “Glad we could assist. Don’t hesitate to reach out.”

Other: “Please call again if you need anything else.”

🔍 Non-Basic Call Script

911 (Fire, Flood, Blood)

• Sorry to hear you're dealing with that. Please hang up immediately and call 911.

Irate/angry/rude resident

If someone is yelling/using profanity:
• I'm sorry you're dealing with this – I'm here to assist you. If you could talk to me in a professional manner we'll get this sorted for you. If you are not able to, I will unfortunately not be able to continue this call.

If someone is making up another emergency:
• I'm sorry that you're dealing with this. But unfortunately your reported issue is not deemed an emergency by your property. Unfortunately if we are unable to continue this call in a professional manner, I will need to disconnect.

If you need to end the call:
• Unfortunately because of the way we're communicating, I won't be able to continue to assist you and will be disconnecting the call. If you would like to call back later, we would be happy to assist. Goodbye.

Resident not contacted by OSS after escalation

• Can I put you on a brief hold while I look up any information on the previous ticket?
• I can see that the technician has not reached out; I will call your property team directly. Otherwise, I can take note of your phone number and give you a call back with an update shortly.

Resident calls back about non-emergency issue

• I'm sorry, your issue is not deemed an emergency by your property. I've already put in a ticket for this issue and your onsite team will address it as soon as they can during regular business hours.
